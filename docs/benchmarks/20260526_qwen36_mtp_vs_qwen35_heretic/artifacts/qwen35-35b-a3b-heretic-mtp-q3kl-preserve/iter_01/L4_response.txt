### Key Fixes:

1. **Added a Lock**: A `threading.Lock` is used to synchronize access to shared state (`jobs` and `results`).

2. **Protected Critical Sections**:
   - The `add_job`, `process_job`, and `get_result` methods now use `with self.lock:` to ensure atomic access.
   - In `process_job`, the check-and-delete sequence is performed under lock to prevent concurrent modifications.

   - The actual job processing (`processor(data)`) happens outside the lock to avoid blocking other threads during potentially long-running operations.

4. **Prevented Duplicate Processing**:
   - Once a job is removed from `self.jobs`, no other thread can process it, ensuring each job is handled exactly once.
